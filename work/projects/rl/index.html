Pac-Man learns to stop panicking

25

A toy agent in a toy maze: exploration shrinks as epochs grow, just like ε-greedy. The red scribble is its path.

I trained agents on Atari Pac-Man for 400 epochs and compared five deep RL algorithms, including DQN, DDQN, Deep SARSA and A2C variants.

Early on, an agent mostly explores at random: that's ε-greedy exploration. Experience replay lets it learn from old mistakes, and target networks stop it chasing its own tail. Slide the epoch and watch the panic turn into a plan.

under the hood
  • Experience replay, target networks, frame stacking, ε-greedy exploration
  • A2C with Generalized Advantage Estimation and temperature annealing, for stabler convergence

The trick that won: memory

Show a network one frame and it can't tell which way a ghost is moving. Stack the last four frames like a flipbook and motion becomes visible.

That one change gave DDQN a 53% higher peak score.
