RL & Multi-agent

Deep reinforcement learning work from the Foundations of Multi-agent AI course, comparing value-based and actor-critic agents on Atari Ms. Pac-Man.

Comparative Analysis of Deep RL Agents in Pac-Man

PyTorchGymnasiumAtari ALEDQN · DDQN · SARSA · A2C

The idea

Ms. Pac-Man is a tough teacher. Points come from pellets, and game-over comes from ghosts you can only dodge if you know where they're heading.

An agent starts out exploring almost at random (the red scribble). With enough experience it learns routes that clear pellets and stay out of trouble (the ink path). The trick that helped most is on the right: one frame can't show which way a ghost is moving, but a stack of the last four frames can.

My contributions

  • Implemented and compared 5 deep RL algorithms on Atari Ms. Pac-Man: DQN, DDQN, DDQN with frame stacking, Deep SARSA and A2C.
  • Trained agents over 400 epochs with experience replay, target networks and ε-greedy exploration, and evaluated each over 100 test episodes.
  • Improved DDQN with frame stacking for temporal awareness, raising the peak score by 53% (2,190 → 3,340).
  • Implemented A2C with Generalized Advantage Estimation and temperature annealing, improving stability and convergence.
  • Compared stochastic and deterministic policy evaluation. Stochastic policies escaped the loops that trapped greedy agents.
