Digital Systems taught me how gates become computers. This project is the fun direction: buttons become music.

embedded projectArduino · C++

A keyboard you can play

20 cm

Click the buttons, or press A S D F G H J. Move your hand (the slider) to change octave.

Seven push buttons map to seven notes, and an Arduino drives a piezo buzzer in real time. The twist is an ultrasonic distance sensor: raise or lower your hand above it and the whole keyboard shifts octave. That's gesture-controlled pitch for the price of a sensor.

under the hood
  • Seven hardware keys for note selection and real-time tone generation
  • Sensor distance mapped to octave scaling
