Arduino Musical Keyboard

ArduinoC++ultrasonic sensorpiezo buzzer

The idea

Seven push buttons are seven notes, like a very small piano. The Arduino reads which key is pressed and drives a piezo buzzer at that note's frequency.

The twist is the ultrasonic sensor. It measures how far away your hand is and maps that distance to an octave multiplier, so moving your hand shifts the whole keyboard up or down. It's gesture-controlled pitch for the price of one sensor.

My contributions

  • Designed an Arduino-powered mini keyboard with seven push-button keys mapped to musical notes and real-time tone generation on a piezo buzzer.
  • Mapped ultrasonic distance readings to an octave multiplier for gesture-controlled pitch changes.
  • Added a resistor stage to attenuate the output signal and keep the buzzer's volume in check.
  • Wrote the Arduino firmware in C++ for input handling, note mapping and tone output.
